## What This Brief Does

In under 5 minutes, every team member knows:

**[Agent A]:** Two urgent items (Garcia financing call, Webb touch) + Johnson monitoring
**[Agent B]:** One critical item (Morales amendment — call listing agent now)
**[Agent C]:** Johnson monitoring only
**Diana:** Two decisions, both by 3pm today, clearly stated

The team doesn't need a standup to know where they stand. The brief replaced the standup.
