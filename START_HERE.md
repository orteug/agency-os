## What This System Is

AgencyOS is the operating layer for this team. It is not software. It is not a chatbot. It is a set of AI specialists organized into Claude projects that help you do your job faster and more consistently.

Each specialist handles one part of the workflow:
- **Orchestrator** — Where everything starts. Route your request here.
- **Lead Qualifier** — Turns new inquiries into actionable lead profiles
- **Property Research** — Deep research briefs on properties and neighborhoods
- **Client Communication** — Drafts emails and texts in the voice of the assigned agent
- **Transaction Coordinator** — Tracks every deadline and document once you're under contract
- **Daily Deal Desk** — Your morning brief. Tells you what matters today.

---

## How to Use It — The Three Things You'll Do Most

### 1. You get a new lead
Tell the orchestrator. It routes to lead qualifier automatically. Check back within the hour — you'll have a qualified lead profile and a drafted first follow-up ready for your review.

### 2. You need a research brief on a property
Tell the orchestrator: the address, the client it's for, and when you need it (especially if there's a showing). Research brief comes back before your showing.

### 3. You need to follow up with a client
Tell the orchestrator: who the client is, what the situation is, and whether it's an email or text. A draft comes back for your review. Read it, edit what doesn't sound like you, send it.

## The One Rule That Matters Most

**Update the case state after anything significant happens.**

Every client interaction, every lender call, every document received — update the relevant fields in the case state. The daily brief is only accurate if the case states are current. If you don't update it, the system can't help you.

This takes 2-3 minutes. It saves you from answering "what's the status on X?" twelve times a week.

## How the Daily Brief Works

Every morning before 9 AM, the daily deal desk produces a brief for the whole team. It tells you:
- What needs your attention today (and what breaks if you ignore it)
- What Diana needs to decide
- How the full pipeline looks
- Any clients who haven't been touched in too long

Read it first. Act on the urgent items before anything else.

## What to Escalate to Diana

When in doubt, escalate. Diana would rather hear about something early than find out it was handled wrong.

**Always escalate to Diana:**
- Pricing conversations (any recommendation you're about to give a client on price)
- Negotiation strategy
- Anything involving legal language or contract interpretation
- A client who is angry or threatening to cancel
- A referral from one of Diana's key relationships
- Anything where you're not sure what the right move is

**Handle yourself (with system support):**
- Scheduling and coordination
- Standard follow-ups
- Research requests
- Document collection on standard items
- First-pass communication drafts (always get agent review)

## Case IDs

Every client and every deal gets a case ID when it enters the system. The format is: `YYMMDD-INITIALS-NNN`

Example: `260512-MW-001` = May 12, 2026, Marcus Webb, first case that day.

Use the case ID when routing anything to the orchestrator — it connects your request to the full case history.

## Connecting Your Tools (Optional — Recommended)

AgencyOS works without any integrations. When you connect your team's tools in Claude.ai, the specialists get smarter automatically.

**The two connectors that matter most:**

**Gmail** — connects in Claude.ai Settings → Integrations
When Gmail is active, specialists read your actual email threads before acting. The client communication specialist reads your last 3 exchanges before drafting a follow-up. The daily deal desk detects stale leads from real email gaps, not just logged dates. The transaction coordinator monitors lender and listing agent threads for risk signals.

**Google Calendar** — connects in Claude.ai Settings → Integrations
When Calendar is active, the transaction coordinator cross-references every contractual deadline against your actual calendar. The daily deal desk includes today's showings and closings in the brief with exact times. The orchestrator adjusts routing priority based on what's actually scheduled today.

**Also available:** Google Drive (existing research documents and contracts), Slack (team coordination threads).

**To connect:**
1. Open Claude.ai → Settings → Integrations
2. Connect Gmail and Google Calendar with your team's Google account
3. The specialists detect the connection automatically — no configuration needed

**What works without connectors:** Everything. Specialists fall back to manually provided case state when no connectors are active. Connecting them upgrades the experience without changing the workflow.
