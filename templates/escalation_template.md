# Escalation Template
## AgencyOS — Diana Queue Format

Every escalation to Diana follows this three-sentence format.

```
ESCALATION: [Case ID] — [Client Name]
Priority: [Immediate | Today | This Week]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Situation: [One sentence. What is happening and why it matters.]

Attempted: [One sentence. What has already been tried or is already known.]

Decision needed: [One sentence. Specifically what Diana needs to decide or do, and by when.]
```

---

## Example

```
ESCALATION: 260512-MG-001 — Garcia / Barton Springs
Priority: Today
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Situation: The financing contingency deadline is tomorrow (May 20) and the lender
has not issued a commitment letter despite being contacted three times this week.

Attempted: Agent called the loan officer May 16, May 18, and May 19 — no response;
agent also emailed and reached the loan officer's assistant, who confirmed the
file is in underwriting.

Decision needed: Do you want to (1) call the lender's manager directly today,
(2) request a 5-day extension from the seller to protect the buyer, or
(3) advise the buyers to prepare for a possible contingency decision — by 3pm today.
```

---

## Rule

If an escalation to Diana cannot be summarized in three sentences, the agent does not have enough information to escalate effectively. Gather the information first, then escalate.
