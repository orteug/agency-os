## Current Market Conditions

**Market Type:** Balanced-to-seller in premium sub-$800k. Buyer-favorable above $1.1M.

**Overall Trend:** Austin has rebalanced from the 2021–2022 peak. Inventory has recovered in most price bands. Days on market have normalized. Multiple-offer situations still occur on well-priced properties under $700k in core neighborhoods but are no longer the default.

**Affordability Pressure:** Interest rates at current levels (6.8–7.2% 30-year conventional as of Q1 2026) have compressed the move-up buyer segment. First-time buyers are the most rate-sensitive. Relocation buyers from higher-cost markets (Chicago, LA, Bay Area) remain active — their purchasing power is strong relative to local buyers.

## Key Pricing Bands — Austin Metro

### Under $500k

- Inventory: moderate
- Days on market: 28–42 days typical
- Typical buyer: first-time buyers, investors, value-focused families
- Market dynamic: competitive for well-priced, updated properties; slower for deferred-maintenance listings
- Key neighborhoods at this range: eastern Travis County, Pflugerville, eastern Cedar Park

### $500k–$800k

- Inventory: tightest band in Austin metro
- Days on market: 18–35 days typical
- Typical buyer: move-up buyers, relocating families, equity-rich sellers
- Market dynamic: most competitive segment; well-priced homes under 25 days still see multiple offers
- Key neighborhoods: Brentwood, Crestview, Allandale, North Loop, portions of Travis Heights, St. Elmo

### $800k–$1.1M

- Inventory: moderate
- Days on market: 35–55 days typical
- Typical buyer: dual-income professional couples, downsizing boomers, relocation executives
- Market dynamic: balanced; buyers have leverage on inspection repairs; sellers still expect within 5% of ask
- Key neighborhoods: Barton Hills (lower end), Tarrytown (entry), Mueller (newer construction), South Congress corridor

### $1.1M–$1.8M

- Inventory: growing; more choice for buyers than in prior years
- Days on market: 45–75 days typical
- Typical buyer: executives, tech leadership, second-home buyers, high-end investors
- Market dynamic: buyer-favorable; price reductions more common; sellers adjusting expectations; negotiation room exists
- Key neighborhoods: Barton Hills, Tarrytown, Old Enfield, Pemberton Heights, Clarksville, Lake Austin adjacent

### $1.8M+

- Inventory: highest relative to demand
- Days on market: 75–120+ days typical
- Typical buyer: very thin pool; often second-home or cash buyers
- Market dynamic: strongly buyer-favorable; significant price correction from 2022 peaks; expect 8–15% below original list on extended listings
- Key neighborhoods: Westlake Hills, Rob Roy, Barton Creek, deep Tarrytown waterfront

## Neighborhood Intelligence — Core Austin

### Travis Heights / Bouldin Creek (78704)

- Character: eclectic, walkable, South Congress corridor, mix of renovated bungalows and new construction
- Buyer profile: design-forward, urban lifestyle, typically DINKs or small families
- Pricing: $700k–$1.4M typical for SFR; new construction pushing upper end
- Trend: stable with slight softening at $1M+; strong rental demand supports investor interest
- Watch: flood zone properties along Bouldin Creek; confirm FEMA map status on any creek-adjacent lot
- School note: Linder Elementary draws families; Travis HS is mixed — private school rates are high in this neighborhood

### Barton Hills (78704 / 78745)

- Character: established, tree-heavy, pool culture, quiet streets with strong community identity
- Buyer profile: families, established professionals, repeat buyers who know Austin well
- Pricing: $650k–$1.6M; wide range based on lot size, updates, and Barton Creek School proximity
- Trend: stable to slight cooling above $1.1M; sub-$900k well-priced properties still move fast
- Watch: Barton Creek Greenbelt access is a genuine amenity premium — properties backing to it price 12–18% higher
- School note: Barton Hills Elementary is the highest-demand elementary in AISD; proximity drives meaningful premium

### Zilker (78704)

- Character: adjacent to Barton Springs, Zilker Park, and Town Lake; premium lifestyle location
- Buyer profile: active outdoor lifestyle, high disposable income, often paying for access not just housing
- Pricing: $850k–$2M+ for SFR; new construction over $2M
- Trend: resilient even in softer market; Zilker Park proximity functions as a permanent amenity floor
- Watch: Barton Springs Road traffic; some streets are significantly noisier than they appear on a map

### Mueller (78723)

- Character: planned community, new urbanist design, diverse household types, strong community events
- Buyer profile: families who want walkability + new construction; first-time buyers who want turnkey
- Pricing: $550k–$1.1M depending on unit type and lot
- Trend: stable; resale is strong relative to purchase price; HOA and community feel are genuine selling points
- Watch: HOA fees are a real cost; confirm with buyers who haven't owned in an HOA before. Still some construction activity in newer sections.

### East Austin (78702)

- Character: rapidly gentrified, mix of longtime residents and newcomers, restaurant/bar culture
- Buyer profile: young professionals, investors, buyers priced out of 78704
- Pricing: $500k–$950k for SFR; wide range based on how far east
- Trend: volatile; interest rate sensitivity higher here than in established neighborhoods; some investor-driven pricing correction in progress
- Watch: East 6th street corridor noise; confirm what's around the property before showing. Gentrification front means block-by-block variation.

### Crestview / Brentwood / Allandale (78757/78756/78731)

- Character: mid-century ranch stock, North Austin, strong school zoning, family-focused
- Buyer profile: families with children, value-focused buyers who know Austin well
- Pricing: $550k–$900k for SFR; Crestview getting compressed as prices push north
- Trend: stable; these neighborhoods hold value well in downturns due to genuine demand from families
- Watch: Brentwood Elementary has a waitlist — confirm enrollment before using school access as a selling point

### Westlake / Eanes ISD (78746)

- Character: established wealth, large lots, strong school district, suburban feel with Hill Country adjacency
- Buyer profile: families prioritizing schools above all, executives, equity-rich relocators
- Pricing: $1.1M–$5M+; floor has risen dramatically; hard to find anything move-in-ready under $1.3M
- Trend: cooling from 2022 peaks but floor is higher than 2019; Eanes ISD premium is real and durable
- Watch: Cedar Bark Lane area has some road noise; confirm lot orientation and street context

## Active Market Signals — May 2026

**What's moving:** Sub-$700k updated properties in walkable zip codes. Relocation buyer activity remains strong Q2 2026.

**What's sitting:** Properties over $1.1M with deferred maintenance. New construction above $1.5M in suburban submarkets. Investment properties with cap rates compressed by high rates.

**Appraisal environment:** Appraisers are conservative. Properties priced aggressively above comp support are being reduced at appraisal. Advise buyers to understand that list price does not guarantee appraisal.

**Inspection environment:** Buyers are using full option periods. Inspection amendment requests are standard — budget for negotiation on any property with roof age, foundation, or HVAC concerns.

**Days on market context:** A property under 21 DOM with no price reduction is genuinely competitive. A property over 45 DOM with a price reduction has leverage available. Always pull DOM history before advising on offer strategy.

## Austin-Specific Risk Flags

**Flood zones:** Bartonville Creek, Shoal Creek, Waller Creek, and portions of Bouldin Creek have expanded FEMA flood map designations since 2021. Always check current FEMA FIRM map for any creek-adjacent property. Do not rely on prior listing disclosure.

**Foundation:** Central Texas soil is expansive clay. Pier-and-beam on pre-1960s properties is normal and manageable. Slab on grade on post-1970s properties — inspect for cracks, monitor irrigation for watering schedule. Never dismiss foundation questions without a structural engineer.

**Galvanized supply lines:** Properties built before 1970 frequently have galvanized supply lines. Replacement cost is $4,000–$12,000 depending on house size. Material fact — disclose early.

**Aluminum wiring:** Properties built 1965–1975 may have aluminum branch circuit wiring. Not a dealbreaker but requires evaluation and potentially COPALUM remediation. Flag to agent.

**Pool permits:** Austin requires permits for pools, decks, and additions. Unpermitted pools are increasingly flagged at inspection. Verify permit history on any listing with visible pool additions or deck structures.

**Short-term rental rules:** City of Austin STR regulations require owner-occupancy for Type 1 permits. Type 2 (non-owner-occupied) permits are no longer issued. Investment buyers planning to STR need to understand the regulatory environment before purchasing.
