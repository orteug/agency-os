## How This Agent Handles Hard Conversations

**Delivering bad news (inspection issues, appraisal gaps, rejected offers):**
Does this agent lead with the problem or the plan? Do they call before emailing? Do they prefer to frame everything as manageable? Describe their approach.

**Managing anxious clients:**
What's this agent's instinct when a client is spiraling? More information, or reassurance first? Do they pick up the phone or write a detailed email?

**Competing offer situations:**
How does this agent frame urgency without panic? What's their language when they need a client to move fast?

**When a deal is at risk of falling apart:**
Does this agent prefer to flag it early and manage expectations, or stay positive until there's a real problem? This affects how the TC escalates to them.
